# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers who arrive through GitHub, search, or shared links and want to understand Asakei's thinking through essays, project notes, and technical observations. They are likely developers, collaborators, or curious peers reading in short focused sessions.

## Product Purpose

Asakei Blog is a personal publishing surface for clear technical writing and project signals. Success means a visitor can quickly understand who Asakei is, read recent notes comfortably, and move naturally to GitHub for code and ongoing work.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly premium. The voice should feel like a well-kept engineering notebook: thoughtful, direct, and intentional without becoming cold.

## Anti-references

Avoid template-like developer portfolios, busy card grids, glowing gradients, terminal cosplay, beige editorial magazine styling, and oversized marketing hero copy. The blog should not look like a SaaS landing page or a generic documentation theme.

## Design Principles

- Let writing be the center of gravity.
- Make the GitHub connection visible without turning the page into a resume.
- Use restraint with a point of view: one strong color system, crisp rhythm, and deliberate empty space.
- Prefer useful details over decorative filler.
- Keep reading comfortable on mobile and desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast at minimum, with body text comfortably above AA. Support reduced motion, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, responsive layout, and readable line lengths for long-form posts.
